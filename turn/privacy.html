Privacy Policy

Turn: Hit the Green • Pergel Games • Effective: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pergel Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the iOS game Turn: Hit the Green ("the App"). We built the App to collect as little data as possible. We do not run our own servers for the App and we do not receive any personal information about you. The only data that leaves your device is handled by Apple and by the advertising partners described below.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise (GPS) location.
  • We do not include any analytics SDKs of our own. The only third-party SDK that collects data is the advertising SDK described in section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device and never sends it to us:

  • Game progress: best score, unlocked levels, remaining hearts and streak statistics.
  • Settings: sound on/off and selected language.
  • A local record of in-app purchases (for example "Remove Ads"), kept as a cache. Apple's purchase receipt remains the source of truth.

Deleting the App removes this data.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to get +1 heart and keep playing. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (Unity / ironSource) mediation, which may request ads from partner ad networks such as Unity Ads, Google AdMob and AppLovin.

When the App loads or shows an ad, these SDKs may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking (see below), and the vendor identifier (IDFV);
  • IP address and coarse (approximate, city or country level) location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, time zone and screen size;
  • ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks and completed rewarded views;
  • diagnostic data such as SDK crashes and ad-loading performance.

Our ad partners use this data to deliver, measure and cap ads, to prevent fraud and, if you allow it, to show ads that are more relevant to you. Their own privacy policies govern how they use it:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource, Unity Ads)
  • Google AdMob
  • AppLovin

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch, iOS asks whether you allow the App to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. In-app purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases, such as extra hearts and removing ads. Apple processes all payments through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the App that a purchase succeeded so we can deliver the item. "Restore Purchases" asks Apple for the items previously bought with your Apple Account. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to the App's Game Center leaderboard together with your Game Center nickname. Apple operates Game Center and controls this data; we do not receive your Apple Account details. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings → Game Center.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected through an ad partner, please contact us and we will help you reach the right partner to have it deleted.

7. Data retention

We do not store personal data on our side, so we have nothing to retain or delete. Locally saved progress stays until you delete the App. Ad partners and Apple keep data according to their own policies linked above.

8. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. Since we hold no personal data, please send such requests directly to the ad partners linked in section 3. You can always opt out of tracking through the ATT setting above and reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings. If you need help, contact us. We reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature or ad partner. The "Effective" date at the top will always show the latest version. If a change is significant, we will mention it in the App's release notes.
