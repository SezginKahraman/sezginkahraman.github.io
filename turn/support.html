How to play

A glowing rope spins around the centre of the ring. Tap anywhere when it is inside the green zone. Hit it right in the middle for a PERFECT, or a little off-centre for a GOOD. Every hit makes the rope faster and the green zone narrower. In One Turn, a miss costs a heart.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between One Turn and Levels?

One Turn is the endless run. It moves straight from one level to the next, your score and streak carry over, and you keep going until you run out of hearts. Levels lets you pick any level you have already reached and practise it. There are no hearts there: a miss simply restarts that level.

I ran out of hearts. Can I continue?

Yes. On the "Continue?" screen you can watch a short rewarded ad for +1 heart, get 3 hearts with an in-app purchase, or start over. Watching ads is always optional.

The ad didn't load and I didn't get my heart

Rewarded ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available. You only get the heart after the ad finishes. If you watched the whole ad and still didn't get the heart, email us with the time it happened.

I bought something but it isn't showing

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. For a permanent item such as removing ads, use Restore Purchases in Settings. For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

The leaderboard uses Game Center. In iOS Settings → Game Center, check that you are signed in, then open the game again. Your best score is sent the next time a run ends.

Why does the game ask to "track" me?

The tracking prompt is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. It decides whether ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads. The game plays exactly the same either way. You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I reset my progress?

Open the menu and tap Reset to start over. Deleting the App also erases all locally saved progress. Purchases tied to your Apple Account can be restored.
